\noindent\textbf{Alignment with Journal Scope and Significance:} \\
The \textit{International Journal of Numerical Methods for Heat \& Fluid Flow} is internationally recognized for advancing state-of-the-art computational algorithms, numerical discretization schemes, and verification benchmarks for fluid flow physics. The 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity problem represents the canonical benchmark for assessing solver accuracy, spatial dispersion, and high-Reynolds numerical stability. Established benchmark datasets, however, have historically stagnated at $Re \le 10{,}000$ (Ghia et al., 1982) or $Re \le 25{,}000$ (Erturk et al., 2005).

In this manuscript, we present a high-resolution finite-difference streamfunction--vorticity ($\psi\text{--}\omega$) framework that directly advances numerical fluid dynamics across several core fronts:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Exact Spectral Poisson Acceleration:} We integrate an exact Discrete Sine Transform (DST-I) Poisson solver scaling at optimal $\mathcal{O}(N^2 \log N)$ complexity, eliminating iterative convergence errors, pressure-velocity decoupling, and numerical drift.
    \item \textbf{Strict Zero Numerical Viscosity ($\nu_{\text{art}} = 0$):} Spatial discretization retains pure second-order central differencing without artificial dissipation for all Reynolds numbers up to $Re = 50{,}000$.
    \item \textbf{Directional Hybrid Regularization at $Re = 100{,}000$:} At extreme cell P\'eclet numbers ($Pe > 195$), an interior directional hybrid model regularizes driven corner singularities with explicitly mapped numerical dissipation tensors.
    \item \textbf{Asymptotic Batchelor Core Verification:} Stationary continuation up to $Re = 100{,}000$ on uniform Cartesian meshes up to $1025 \times 1025$ ($1{,}050{,}625$ nodes) resolves primary and secondary eddy cascades, providing numerical evidence qualitatively consistent with Batchelor's core homogenization theorem ($\omega_0 \approx -2.54$, $x_c \to 0.50000$).
    \item \textbf{Finite-Window Unsteady Marching on $513 \times 513$ Meshes:} Physical time-accurate marching resolves supercritical Hopf bifurcations to periodic vortex shedding at $Re = 10{,}000$ ($St = 0.6249 \pm 0.0416$), amplitude-modulated multi-frequency dynamics at $Re = 15{,}000$ ($St = 0.5364 \pm 0.0278$), and finite-window spectral transitions up to $Re = 100{,}000$.
    \item \textbf{Open Science and Benchmark Reproducibility:} Thirty (30) verified full 2D coordinate flow field datasets (23 steady continuation and 7 unsteady telemetry records) with cryptographic SHA-256 checksums are openly archived under the MIT license.
\end{enumerate}
