\noindent\textbf{Title:} A High-Resolution Finite-Difference Benchmark for Steady Continuation and Hopf Bifurcation Dynamics up to Extreme Reynolds Numbers ($Re = 100,000$) in 2D Lid-Driven Cavity Flows

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Author:} Kartikeya Gangwar

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Highlights (Each bullet strictly $\le 85$ characters including spaces):}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Exact $\mathcal{O}(N^2 \log N)$ DST-I Poisson solver eliminates iterative residual drift.
    \item Pure central differencing achieves zero artificial viscosity up to $Re = 50,000$.
    \item Localized hybrid scheme regularizes corner shocks at $Re = 100,000$ on $1025 \times 1025$.
    \item Symmetric core convergence confirms Batchelor's asymptotic theorem at high $Re$.
    \item Time-accurate $513 \times 513$ marching maps Hopf bifurcation cascades to $Re = 100,000$.
\end{itemize}
