# Research Highlights

**Journal:** *Computers & Fluids* (Elsevier)  
**Title:** High-Resolution Finite-Difference Benchmarks for the 2D Lid-Driven Cavity Flow up to $Re = 100,000$: Exact Discrete Sine Transform, Batchelor Core Slices, and Hopf Bifurcation Cascades  
**Author:** Kartikeya Gangwar  

---

### Highlights (Strictly $\le 125$ characters per bullet including spaces):

1. Zero-dissipation FDM pairing pure central differencing with an exact $\mathcal{O}(N^2 \log N)$ DST-I Poisson engine.
   *(106 characters)*
2. Steady continuation up to $Re = 100,000$ on $1025^2$ mesh verifying Batchelor's core theorem ($\omega_0 = -2.5418$).
   *(113 characters)*
3. Spalding–Patankar hybrid differencing arrests corner shocks ($Pe \gg 2$) while preserving $\nu_{\text{num}} = 0$ in the core.
   *(118 characters)*
4. Time-accurate marching maps supercritical Hopf, 2-torus, and limit-cycle regimes up to $Re = 100,000$ ($St = 0.5128$).
   *(115 characters)*
5. Open-source release of 28 high-$Re$ flow field tensors, limit-cycle orbits, and automated visualization pipelines.
   *(114 characters)*
