\noindent {\LARGE \textbf{Research Highlights}}

\vspace{1em}
\noindent \textbf{Manuscript Title:} A High-Resolution Finite-Difference Benchmark for Steady Continuation and Hopf Bifurcation Dynamics up to Extreme Reynolds Numbers ($Re = 100,000$) in 2D Lid-Driven Cavity Flows

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{Author:} Kartikey Singh

\noindent \textbf{Affiliation:} Department of Mathematics, University of Delhi, Delhi, 110007, India

\noindent \textbf{Target Journal:} \textit{Computers \& Fluids} (Elsevier)

\vspace{1.5em}
\hrule
\vspace{1.5em}

\begin{itemize}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{0.8em}
    \item Exact $\mathcal{O}(N^2 \log N)$ DST-I Poisson solver eliminates iterative residual drift.
    \item Pure central differencing achieves zero numerical viscosity up to $Re = 50,000$.
    \item Localized hybrid scheme regularizes corner shocks at $Re = 100,000$ on $1025 \times 1025$.
    \item Asymptotic core vorticity plateau confirms Batchelor's theorem at high $Re$.
    \item Time-accurate $513 \times 513$ marching resolves limit cycles and unsteady dynamics.
\end{itemize}
