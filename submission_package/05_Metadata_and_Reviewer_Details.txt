Full Title:
A High-Resolution Finite-Difference Benchmark for Steady Continuation and Hopf Bifurcation Dynamics up to Extreme Reynolds Numbers (Re = 100,000) in 2D Lid-Driven Cavity Flows

Short Running Title:
Extreme-Re Cavity Flow Benchmark & Hopf Dynamics

================================================================================
2. ABSTRACT (249 words - Strictly <= 250 words limit)
===============================================================================
The 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity problem is a canonical CFD benchmark. While classical studies focus on Re <= 10,000, higher-Re regimes lack open tensor datasets and unified bifurcation records. We present a reference-grade finite-difference suite across steady and unsteady regimes from Re = 100 to Re = 100,000. The numerical engine couples an exact O(N^2 log N) Discrete Sine Transform (DST-I) Poisson solver, eliminating iterative residuals, with vectorized Alternating Direction Implicit marching and parameter homotopy. For Re <= 50,000, pure second-order central differencing is strictly preserved (nu_num = 0 everywhere). At Re = 100,000 (Pe = 195.3), an interior Spalding--Patankar hybrid differencing scheme arrests corner singularity dispersion while maintaining well-bounded numerical dissipation in the core (nu_num_excess / nu <= 0.99 on 513 x 513 and 0.24 on 1025 x 1025). On meshes up to 1025 x 1025, steady continuation resolves primary and corner eddies, matching Ghia et al. and Erturk et al. At Re = 50,000 and 100,000, solutions reproduce Batchelor's core vorticity plateau (omega_0 ~ -2.54) with symmetric mid-plane centering (x_c -> 0.50000). Physical marching on 513 x 513 grids (t = 16.0-22.0) resolves the primary Hopf limit cycle (St = 0.6249 +/- 0.0416 at Re = 10,000), captures modulated multi-frequency dynamics at Re = 15,000 (St = 0.5364 +/- 0.0278), and characterizes nonlinear shedding at Re = 25,000 (St = 0.0953 +/- 0.0281), Re = 50,000 (St = 0.2581 +/- 0.0300), and Re = 100,000 (St = 0.1908 +/- 0.0379). Datasets, tools, and animations are openly archived.
